# Broilers-Resistome
Characterizing the effects of a probiotic and an essential oils blend on the broiler resistome
Assessing the impact of a Bacillus-based probiotic and an essential oil blend on broiler excreta resistome composition in comparison with a basal diet and a conventional AGP treatment. We hypothesized that dietary intervention would differentially change ARGs diversity and structure of broilers’ excreta microbiome. This work provides insight into the functional consequences of antibiotic-free dietary strategies on AMR dynamics in modern poultry production.
